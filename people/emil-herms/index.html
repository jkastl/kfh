The 1985 book lists only Ron, printed as died 6 Apr. 1980. The 1986 supplement's unnumbered "Children:" section under this heading says "RON still living", gives the death date 6 Apr. 1981 for his wife Anette George, and adds CYNTHIA LEE (b. 3 Feb., d. 4 Feb. 1953). Both readings of Ron's death are kept. The supplement also gives the marriage as 24 Sept. 1945 instead of 15 Sept. 1945.
