  • He married Delois Sunny Tarron 21 November 1977. — p. 53

The heading names only Roxie Rose Thompson. The entry gives both marriages (Roxie, 3 August 1964, divorced 15 May 1977; Delois Sunny Tarron, 21 November 1977) before "Their children:"; all four children were born 1964-1973, during the first marriage, and are kept with the heading's family. No children of the second marriage are printed.
