Children in printed order. Frank Kohoutek (b. 1915) and Ludvick, who is Louis Peter Rerucha in his own entry (b. 1913), were adopted on 9 July 1921; the book calls them natural brothers and names no other parents for them. Frank kept his original surname, and the 1986 supplement's lineages run "(Frank Kohoutek, Elizabeth Kastl Rerucha, Jacob)" through this adoption; the book does not mention any other marriage of Elizabeth's. Zita (b. 1922) is listed after Regina (b. 1924). Elizabeth's death is 1965 in the 1985 book and 1968 in the 1986 supplement.
