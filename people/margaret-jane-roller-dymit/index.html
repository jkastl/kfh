The book says: "Two children were born to first marriage. Patrick married Margaret Jane Roller Dymit—5 June 1971, Omaha, Nebraska." Its list under "The family of Patrick Kastl and Jane Roller Dymit" numbers all three children together; Gregory Patrick and Daniel Lee are placed here with the first marriage, Mark Robert with the second.
