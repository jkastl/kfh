Patricia Louise (b. 1947) and Jeanne Elizabeth (b. 1949) were Amy's daughters by her first husband, Captain Herbert G. Lierman (d. 13 December 1949, Osaka, Japan), and were adopted by Ernest Mares in Erie, Pennsylvania, September 1965. The book lists all five under "Their children", so they are kept in this family; their birth surname is not printed.
