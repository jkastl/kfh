Eva Kastl

What the book records

NotedPossibly a daughter of Thomas and Maria. Named as a sister in Jacob Kastl's obituary, but the authors found nothing to verify her.p. 7 p. 44
As printed
  • There may have been a daughter, Eva. She is mentioned in the obituary of Jacob Kastl as a sister who preceded him in death. To date, nothing has been found to verify this. — p. 7
  • Mr. Kastl had six brothers, Peter, Thomas, Joseph, Andrew, John, and Wencl, and one sister, Eva, all of whom passed away prior to the death of Grandpa Kastl. — p. 44
