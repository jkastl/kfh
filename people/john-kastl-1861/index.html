Parents

Wenzel Kastl b. 1831 and Barbara Blachnik d. 1909

The 1985 book prints "The family of John Kastl and Mary" with six children. The 1986 supplement says John never married and that the family should be "The family of Martin Kastl"; the six children are therefore placed with Martin, and this marriage is kept only as the 1985 reading.
