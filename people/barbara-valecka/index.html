The photo captioned "Andrew Kastl family" (p. 58) names "Top—Mary, Rose, Frances, Barbara. Bottom—Andrew, John, Barbara, Agnes, Emma, and Anna." It is attached to Andrew, to Barbara Valecka (taken as the Barbara in the bottom row), and to the children named; the Mary is taken to be Mary born 1884, since the first Mary died in 1881, and the other Barbara to be Barbara Elizabeth.
