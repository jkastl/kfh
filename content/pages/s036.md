### The family of Mary E. Mares and John W. Ptacek

MARY E. MARES (Barbara Kastl Mares, John) born 5 April 1888, Saunders County, Nebraska, daughter of Barbara Kastl and Albert Mares, died 19 February 1963, and was buried at Dwight, Nebraska. She married John W. Ptacek 27 October 1913, at Emporia, Kansas. He was born 12 May 1890, Saunders County, Nebraska, son of Vaclav Ptacek and Josephine Kradluber, died 21 January 1965, and was buried at Dwight. John and Mary lived on farms near Emporia and Miller, Kansas, and in 1917 moved to Nebraska, living near Ulysses and Garrison. In 1928 they settled near Dwight
Their children:

1. LORETTA — (b) 27 Jan. 1917. (m) Warren E. Brighton—4 Sept. 1940.
2. RICHARD J. — (b) 22 Nov. 1919. (m) Marie Ella Roth. (No Children)
3. DONALD L. — (b) 4 Dec. 1926. (m) Helen Dobrusky—6 June 1950.
4. GERALD — (b) 10 Dec. 1929. (d) 17 Oct. 1930.
