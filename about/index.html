About this site

Kastl Family History was compiled by Evelyn Coufal and Francis Vech and printed in 1985 by Catholic Quote of Valparaiso, Nebraska. A Supplementary Section followed in 1986. This site presents the book page by page, with its family lists turned into linked records you can browse as a tree or a table.

A record as of 1985–1986. Everything here comes from the 1985 book and its 1986 supplement. Births, marriages and deaths since then are not included, and people the book describes as living, or as living in a certain place, are described as they were in 1985–86.

The only exception: one person born after the supplement, added by the site owner and marked “Unsourced”.

What is here

  • The book: all 76 scanned pages, each beside a transcription of its text.
  • The family tree and a table of everyone: 1285 people, from the earliest Kastl ancestors through the seven brothers, their half-sister Anna and all their descendants named in the book.

How it was made

  1. The scans come from a microfilm copy held by the Family History Library; its library stamps and handwritten call numbers appear on a few pages and are noted separately from the book’s own text.
  2. Every page was transcribed from the images, then proofread a second time, independently, against the images. Names, dates and numbers were also cross-checked against the scan’s machine-read text.
  3. Each fact on a person’s page is tied to the page it comes from, with the exact words the book uses under “As printed”. The site is rebuilt only if every one of those quotations still appears, word for word, in the transcription.

Reading conventions

  • Spellings are the book’s own. The authors wrote that “no attempt has been made to standardize the spelling of names”: Josef and Joseph, Jan and John, Marsalek and Marselek all appear. Typing slips are kept too, marked [sic] where they might look like transcription errors.
  • When the book disagrees with itself, every version is shown with its source, marked “The sources differ”. The 1986 Supplement often corrects the 1985 pages; both readings are kept.
  • [?] marks a reading that the scan does not make certain.
  • Nothing has been added from outside the book, except the few entries marked “Unsourced”.

The book’s own words

It is hoped that most of the information here is correct, or nearly so. Apologies are made for any errors, omissions, or inconsistencies.

Preface, p. 2

Transcribed from Kastl Family History by Evelyn Coufal and Francis Vech (printed 1985, Catholic Quote, Valparaiso, Nebraska) and its 1986 Supplementary Section. Names, dates and spellings are given as the book prints them.
