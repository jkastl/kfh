The heading and lineage spell her name Colleta; the child list and the text also use Colletta. Curtis Allan is printed as born 25 February 1955, three months before the marriage of 25 May 1955; both are kept as printed. The paragraph under this heading also records Colletta's second marriage, to Dwaine Kubicek in 1975, before "Their children:"; the children (born 1955 and 1956) are placed with Gene Pomeroy, as the heading has it. The grandchildren printed after each child's line ("Children: …") are kept as notes on Curtis Allan and Kathleen Marie.
