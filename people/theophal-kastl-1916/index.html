The book prints one heading for both of Theophal's marriages ("The family of Theophal Kastl and Josephine Kriz (2) Virginia McCaston") and a single list, which follows "married (2) Virginia McCaston. Their children:". No marriage dates are printed. John H. is printed as the "Adopted son of Virginia McCaston"; the book does not say which marriage Jerone A. and Darryl V. were born to. All three are kept together here, with the second marriage that the list follows.
