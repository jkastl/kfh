WENZEL and "No Name" are both printed as born 15 Nov. 1877. The 1867 ship's records list a son Wenzel, age 6 months; the child list has no son of that age, and its WENZEL is born in 1877 ("Wenzel 2" in the 1880 census). Both are kept as printed.
