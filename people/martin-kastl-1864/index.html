Printed in the 1985 book as "The family of John Kastl and Mary", whose text says of John: "It is believed he moved his family to California in the 1900s". The 1986 supplement says: "The family of John Kastl and Mary is an error. It should be: The family of Martin Kastl. This family moved to San Francisco, CA." The children are placed here, with Martin, as the supplement says; no mother is named for them.
