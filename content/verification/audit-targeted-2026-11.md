# Targeted audit, November 2026

This audit checked 88 records against the **page images**, not just the transcriptions. The records came from four lists:

- **A.** 43 people whose page shows "The sources differ".
- **B.** 6 plausibility warnings.
- **C.** 13 allow-listed exceptions.
- **D.** 26 family structures that were judgement calls.

For each item I located the cited text in the 300 dpi scan (`work/rend/sNNN.jpg`). The 1986 supplement page s071 was read from `media/pages/s071.jpg`. I cropped the relevant column and read the value, quote or surrounding family text off the image. Each scan was read once for all the items that cite it. The rules applied are those in `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/EXTRACTION.md`. The book is the only source. Disagreements are recorded, not resolved. Spellings stay as printed.

Verdicts: **OK** means the data matches the scan. **fixed** means an extraction slip was corrected (see "Fixes made"). **needs decision** means the data matches the scan, but the owner should decide how it is presented or structured.

## Fixes made

All six fixes are in `content/people/john.json`, on marriage facts. Each person had several marriages. The later ones carried "(2)" or "(3)", but the first had no "(1)". The build treats several marriage facts as numbered only when every value starts with "(" (see `numbered` / `has_conflict` in `tools/build.py`). Without the "(1)", these consecutive marriages were shown as "The sources differ". Other records already follow this convention, for example Ron Herms's "(1) Annette George". Only the value changed. Quotes and sources are untouched.

| person | before | after |

| vivian-josephine-liska-1925 | `Robert P. Binkley, 3 May 1945` | `(1) Robert P. Binkley, 3 May 1945` |

## Needs a decision

1. **eleanor-kastl-1933, marriage.** The s074 supplement prints "She married Harvey Schroeder." with no date. The second marriage fact turns that silence, plus the children's 1950 birth date, into a reading that the book does not print. The real tension, marriage 9 Feb. 1958 against children born 1950 and 1951, is already in the family note and the plausibility warning. *Recommendation:* add the s074 quote as a further source on the 1958 marriage fact (it confirms the spouse), and drop the separate "no date given" fact. This would remove a "sources differ" that is not a disagreement.
2. **lawrence1917-frances and theophal1916-\*, consistency.** Both entries read "married X. ... married (2) Y. Their children:". Lawrence's only child is placed with the first wife, named in the heading. Theophal's children are placed with the second wife, after whom the list follows. The book settles neither. *Recommendation:* choose one rule. The simplest is to follow the heading when it names one spouse, and the list's position when it names both, which is roughly what the data does now. Then state that rule in `docs/EXTRACTION.md`, so that future units are consistent. No data change is needed unless the owner prefers otherwise.

## Decisions (2026-11-27)
1. **Eleanor Kastl's marriage:** the second fact was an inference, not a printed reading, so it was
   removed. The s074 line "She married Harvey Schroeder." is now a further source on the dated
   1958 fact. The 1950/1958 tension stays visible as a plausibility warning and in the family note.
2. **Which marriage a child list belongs to:** the rule is written into docs/EXTRACTION.md (the
   heading's single spouse; otherwise the marriage the list follows). The data already follows it,
   so nothing changed.
