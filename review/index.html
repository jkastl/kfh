Book-caused exceptions to the checks

  • s076 · william-louis-rerucha-1954: The 1986 supplement prints "WILLIAM LOUIS RERUCHA (Louis Peter, or Ludvik Rerucha, Elizabeth Kastl Rerucha, Jacob)".
