This is Frances's second marriage; her first husband, Otto Dvorak, died in 1918, and no children are listed for that marriage. ERNEST is printed "(Son of Anton)" and born 4 May 1915, before this marriage of 1920; the book lists him among "Their children" and does not say who his mother was. He is kept in this family as printed, with the surname Janak implied by "Son of Anton".
