## John Kastl

*(Page 31 Addition)*

### The family of Edmund Kantor and Phyllis Miriovsky

1. JANE MARIE — (b) 11 Dec. 1944.
2. DOROTHY KAY — (b) 28 Mar. 1946.
3. BARBARA BETH — (b) 31 Dec. 1949 (m) Jerry Arden Johnson—7 Sept. 1974.
4. JERRY FRANCIS — (b) 14 Mar. 1953 (m) Nancy Smythe.

1. ERIN KAY — (b) 21 Feb. 1979.
